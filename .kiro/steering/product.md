# Product

**SaaSSy** is a SaaS marketing landing page for a free mobile productivity app.

## Purpose
A single-page marketing site that promotes the SaaSSy mobile app — a productivity tool that helps users plan, create, and stay organized from their phone.

## Key Messaging
- Tagline: "Your day, simplified — right in your pocket"
- Free app, available worldwide
- Social proof: 1.2M+ users, 4.9 average rating, Product Hunt featured

## Target Audience
Individual productivity users and teams looking for a simple, mobile-first task management and planning solution.

## Page Sections (in order)
1. Hero — headline, CTA, social proof badges, marquee
2. ValueStatement
3. HowItWorks
4. ProductivityToolkit
5. FeaturesMatrix
6. VisualShowcase
7. Testimonials
8. StatsMarquee
9. PlansOverview
10. CtaHero
11. Footer
